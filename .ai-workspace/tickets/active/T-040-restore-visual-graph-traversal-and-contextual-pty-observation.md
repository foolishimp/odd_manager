### Binding ABG 5 mutable-workspace law

The Product-owner correction on 2026-09-02 is binding for this outcome:

- ABG 5 is an immutable event/occurrence framework over a mutable workspace;
- the workspace remains the mutable reality carrier;
- stable workspace binding is not an immutable cut of workspace content;
- pre-effect and post-effect observations may describe bounded workspace state;
- later observations do not rewrite an earlier occurrence and do not imply a
  new workspace binding or basis fork while binding authority is unchanged;
- every reapplication creates fresh immutable history over the evolving
  subject; and
- a projection may report currentness or staleness but cannot replace the
  workspace or manufacture rollback/snapshot semantics.

The old attempt to create or reconstruct immutable workspace cuts is explicitly
superseded and is a hard non-closure condition.

### Four truth planes

| Plane | Owner and source | What the UI may claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Declaration topology | admitted GTL/Program/GraphFunction and Product overlay carriers | nodes, typed composition, ports, vectors, groups, and overlay annotations |
| Immutable execution history | exact ABG events, occurrence identities, event frontier, and replay | traversed, active under the declared frontier, closed, retried, refused, or last observed |
| Mutable workspace reality | stable workspace binding plus bounded pre/post/external observations and currentness law | observed state at a coordinate, changed-since relation, stale, or materially unknown |
| Actor session capability | actor-process/transport identity plus separately admitted live read/attach/input or archive relation | watch, attach, interact, archived-only, exited, revoked, unavailable, or unknown |

No plane may fill a missing relation in another plane.
