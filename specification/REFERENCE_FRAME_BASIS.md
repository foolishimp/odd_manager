## Frame Configuration

frames and activates only the material specialist and Product-testing frames
for each bounded work item. Activations bind an exact subject, basis, affected
relations, evidence boundary, actor capability, inherited authority, and return
condition.

- the Worker constructs and self-reviews one bounded candidate;

- overlap in evidence does not collapse actor identity or authority.
